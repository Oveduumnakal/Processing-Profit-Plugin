# Processing Profit

A [RuneLite](https://runelite.net/) plugin that finds profitable item **processing** — the multi-step
conversion chains that turn raw materials into finished products (cutting gems, cooking food, smithing
bars, fletching, crafting jewellery, and so on). It prices every step against live Grand Exchange data,
accounts for success rates, skill/quest gating, buy limits and GE tax, and shows you what is worth making
right now — either from the items you already hold or across the whole game.

**Informational only.** The plugin displays, sorts and filters. It never clicks, types, or fills in
Grand Exchange prices or quantities for you. Every trade is yours to make.

## Features

- **Browse** every known processing recipe, ranked by profit, GP/hr, XP/hr, ROI, success %, level, volume,
  or how many you can make now.
- **On-hand** — what your current bank and inventory can be turned into profitably.
- **Watch** — pin the recipes you care about; the list persists between sessions.
- **Shop** — generate an aggregated shopping list (recursive bill of materials) for a target quantity,
  with buy-limit-aware 4-hour buying windows.
- **Detail view** — click any recipe for a full breakdown: per-input cost, tools, yield-adjusted output,
  GE tax, success-weighted net profit, throughput (gp/hr · xp/hr · ROI), and per-input break-even prices.
- **Alt-hover tooltip** over inventory and bank items, showing the best chain that item feeds.
- **Sourcing modes** — value ingredients as *On-hand* (from your stock), *Buy to order* (all bought), or
  *Hybrid* (held + shortfall bought).
- **Live gating** — profit and success math run at your real skill levels and quest completion; locked
  recipes can be hidden, greyed, or shown.
- **Success modifiers** — cooking gauntlets, cooking/max cape, Hosidius range diary tier and the
  jeweller's chisel are auto-detected (or forced on/off) and folded into the success math.
- **Warnings** — buy-limit throttling and low-GE-volume badges flag illiquid or slow-to-source recipes.
- **Members / F2P filter** — restrict the list to free-to-play or members-only recipes.

## Configuration

Settings live under **Processing Profit** in the RuneLite plugin panel.

**Profit calculation**
- *Sourcing mode* — default valuation assumption (On-hand / Buy to order / Hybrid). The sidebar selector
  overrides it for the session.
- *Valuation* — GE market, or the Ironman (no-GE) lens (v2 placeholder).
- *Apply 2% GE tax* — subtract the Grand Exchange sales tax from product value.
- *Efficiency %* — fraction of theoretical actions/hr actually achieved (banking, travel overhead).
- *Price refresh (s)* — how often live prices refresh from the wiki 5-minute averages.

**Display**
- *Min profit (gp)*, *Min GP/hr*, *Min volume* — hide recipes below these thresholds.
- *Show hover tooltip* — draw the best-chain tooltip on alt-hover.

**Success modifiers** — per-modifier toggles (auto-detect / on / off) for the jeweller's chisel, cooking
gauntlets, cooking cape and Hosidius range.

The sidebar also carries session-only filters: search, skill, gating (hide/grey/show locked),
members/F2P, sort key and card/compact density.

## Data sources

- **[OSRS Wiki Real-time Prices API](https://prices.runescape.wiki/)** — live Grand Exchange buy/sell
  prices and trade volumes (5-minute and 1-hour averages), and the item id/name/buy-limit mapping. This
  API is a RuneLite / OSRS Wiki partnership and the standard source for live prices. The plugin follows
  its acceptable-use policy: it sends a descriptive `User-Agent`, caches results, and polls no more often
  than the configured refresh interval.
- **[OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/)** recipe, success-rate and modifier data — extracted
  offline into a bundled snapshot (`recipes.json`, `success_params.json`, `success_modifiers.json`,
  `failure_outputs.json`) that ships with the plugin, so there is no recipe fetch at runtime. The snapshot
  is regenerated by the scripts in [`tools/`](tools/) and refreshed via the `refresh-data.yml` workflow
  (manual or monthly, opening a pull request).
